Privacy policy

Clip Publisher · last updated 11 November 2026

Clip Publisher is a private tool used by one operator to publish short videos to the operator's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts (and, later, TikTok accounts). It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (through Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API): permission to upload videos to the operator's channels, read those channels' videos, and read their analytics (views, watch time, likes, shares and viewers' countries in aggregate).
  • Instagram (through Meta's Graph API): permission to publish Reels to the operator's accounts and read those posts' insights.

What it stores

Access tokens are kept on the operator's own computer and as encrypted secrets in the operator's private code repository. Post links and aggregate view numbers of the operator's own posts are kept in that repository to measure what works. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising.

Google user data

Clip Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from YouTube is used only to publish the operator's videos and measure their performance.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Facebook's Business integrations settings (Meta). Stored tokens are then deleted.
